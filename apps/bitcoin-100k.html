Je už Bitcoin za 100k?

Is Bitcoin at $100,000 yet? Yes or no. That's it. No chart, no portfolio, no noise — just the answer.

₿

What does it do?

It checks the Bitcoin price every 15 minutes and shows you one word: Ano (yes) or Ne (no). Big, clear, impossible to miss. The browser tab icon changes with the answer. Leave the page open and the moment it flips to yes, you get confetti and a celebration sound; if it drops back, a sad trumpet.

No chart. No portfolio tracker. No 24h volume. Just the one answer you actually care about when Bitcoin is making headlines.

Why $100K specifically?

When Bitcoin was approaching that level in late 2024, I kept opening CoinMarketCap to check. Not to trade — just to know. The page was full of things I didn't need. I wanted one answer, instantly, without loading a full exchange dashboard. So I made that.

The name

"Je už Bitcoin za 100k?" is Czech for "Is Bitcoin at 100K yet?" — exactly the question I kept asking. The site lives at jeuzbitcoinza100k.cz and is in Czech only.

Is it still running?

Yes. It's the oldest project on this site, and it still answers the same question every 15 minutes. If you'd want a version for other coins or other price levels, tell me.

The idea

It was November 2024. Bitcoin was somewhere around $95K and climbing. I kept refreshing CoinMarketCap every hour — not because I needed the full chart, not because I was about to make any moves. I just needed to know: has it crossed $100K yet?

CoinMarketCap is a great site. But when all you want is a yes or no, it's a lot of page. So I opened ChatGPT and described what I actually needed: fetch the price, compare it to 100,000, show the result. Big and obvious.

Building it

This one came before Claude Code was part of my workflow. ChatGPT wrote the page, I put it on GitHub Pages under its own domain, and it went from first commit to finished over one weekend (23–24 November 2024). A problem you'd normally just accept and work around became something I could actually solve.

"Some ideas have a shelf life of hours. AI lets you act on them before the moment passes."

Where it's going

Nowhere big. It's Czech-only and fixed to the $100K threshold, and it does that one job. If you'd want a multi-coin version, send me a message.
