Random Thoughts Podcast

Every Friday, one random thought, followed all the way down. Two characters, Jan and Zuza, untangle the strangest true stories from history, science and the human mind.

Listen on Spotify
🗓️ Since Jan 2025 🎧 In English 🤖 AI voices 📅 New episodes on Fridays
🎙️

What is it?

Why did people dance in the streets of Strasbourg in 1518? Could laughter shut down a school? Each week, Jan or Zuza brings a rabbit hole, the other one brings the doubts, and together they untangle the strangest true stories from history, science and the human mind.

Every Season 2 episode comes with its sources.

Season 1 and Season 2

Season 1 ran from January to May 2025: nine episodes, voiced by AI from the start and made by hand, each one hours of work. Then the show went quiet.

Season 2 started in October 2026 with a new workflow, and new episodes come out on Fridays.

Who does what in Season 2

Ideas by a human. Research and scripts written by AI, reviewed by a human. All voices and the cover art are AI-generated; Jan and Zuza are characters. Sources in every episode.

The workflow

An idea becomes a research brief, then a script. Before an episode goes out, the claims in the script are checked against their sources, and a human listens to the finished episode.
