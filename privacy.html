Last updated: 5 October 2026

What data we collect

We have no user accounts and no login system. Nothing is sent to Google, Cusdis or any analytics service until you have made a choice in the cookie banner. What we process, and when:

  • Your cookie choice — stored in your browser's localStorage under ck_consent and ck_vid (a random visitor ID used to version your consent record). This data never leaves your device.
  • Visit statistics (only if you accept analytics) — Google Analytics 4, loaded through Google Tag Manager. It records the pages you view (the page address including any parameters, and the page you came from) and five clicks: the apps overview, the idea chatbot, the contact e-mail link, the story link and the Claude link. Google also receives your IP address and the technical details every browser sends. Google's cookies _ga and _ga_… are set. We do not use advertising, remarketing or audience features. [TO BE COMPLETED BEFORE PUBLISHING: data retention period set in Google Analytics.]
  • Comments (only if you accept functional cookies) — to show comments, your browser asks cusdis.com for them, so Cusdis receives your IP address. If you write a comment, the name, e-mail address and text you enter go to Cusdis.
  • Ideas you send through the chatbot — your idea and problem description, and, only if you type them, your name and e-mail address. They are sent by Web3Forms to our e-mail inbox when you press “Send my idea”.
  • Newsletter — your e-mail address, sent to Buttondown when you submit the newsletter form on the home page. Buttondown sends you an e-mail asking you to confirm, and every newsletter has an unsubscribe link.
  • Hosting — the site is delivered by GitHub Pages, so GitHub processes your IP address and request details to serve pages. We do not receive or evaluate these logs.
  • Fonts — Outfit and Inter are served from this site; your browser does not contact Google for fonts.
  • QR Clock page — the QR Clock page loads a small code library from the jsDelivr network (cdn.jsdelivr.net), so jsDelivr receives your IP address when you open that page.

Why we process it, and for how long

  • Analytics, comments and the newsletter rest on your consent. You can withdraw it at any time (see “Your rights”).
  • Ideas you send are read to answer you and to decide what to build. We keep them for as long as needed for that and for at most 12 months, or delete them earlier on request. [TO BE COMPLETED BEFORE PUBLISHING: how long Web3Forms keeps the messages it relays.]
  • Newsletter addresses stay with Buttondown until you unsubscribe.

Analytics (optional)

If you accept analytics, Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics 4 are loaded and set the cookies _ga and _ga_… (Google's default lifetime is up to two years). Without your consent neither is loaded.

Functional (optional)

These enable the comment sections powered by Cusdis, an open-source comment system. Comments are loaded from cusdis.com only after you accept this category; when you choose to write a comment, a script from cusdis.com is loaded and may set its own cookies. See the Cusdis Privacy Policy for details.

Marketing

We do not use advertising or remarketing cookies.

Your rights

You can withdraw or change your cookie preferences at any time by clicking the cookie settings button that appears in the bottom-right corner of any page. Clearing your browser's localStorage removes the stored preferences.

You have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to its processing, to receive it in a portable form, and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting what was done before. Write to contactme@resonantlabs.online. For anything held by a provider above (for example your newsletter subscription), you can also use its own tools. You may complain to the data protection authority in your country.

Changes to this policy

If we add new third-party services or change what we collect, we will update this page and its date. If a change needs your consent, we will increment the consent version, which means you'll be asked again on your next visit.
